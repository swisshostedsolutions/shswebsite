Projects

What we are working on.

Two projects are at the center of our work — both open, both on Swiss soil.

Self-hosting

Hosted NextCloud

Self-service recipes and Infrastructure-as-Code implementations that let you set up and run your own NextCloud instance — documented step by step and fully open source.

In a later step we will also offer NextCloud as a managed service: we host your instance for you, with selectable SLA levels — from basic operation to comprehensive care.

Status: in progress — the recipes are being extended continuously.

Transparency

Luegid ane

"Luegid ane" is Swiss dialect and means "look at it". The project monitors and documents the spending and contracting of the public sector in Switzerland.

To do so, we evaluate the procurement platform simap.ch and build a knowledge graph (Neo4j) about NGOs and the people involved — making connections visible that would otherwise stay hidden.

Status: early stage — the project description is being refined continuously.
