Websites

We exclusively build websites and web applications that do not rely on so-called CDNs (Content Delivery Networks) and hosted fonts or icons. Everything on your pages will be provided locally from your own servers, or hosted by us on our servers. We also do not use cookies. There are no analytics tools with us. The pages or applications created by us will not bother your esteemed visitors with warnings and settings regarding so-called cookies. We follow the principle of considering mobile devices as the starting point of our development and optimizing your pages for them. Of course, we cannot meet every customer request with this approach, as there are sometimes requirements that cannot be implemented within this framework. We are aware that this means we cannot serve a segment of potential customers. While this can be painful, our main concerns are the features of our solutions described above. This page has been built according to these principles.

Guided Hosting

The offerings for cloud and hosting are incredibly diverse today, and there is a solution available in the cloud or through so-called SAAS (Software as a Service) for every use case. Examples include major cloud providers like Google, Microsoft, and Amazon. For individuals who rely on a certain level of IT for business reasons, such solutions often become overly complex. The tradesperson, the law firm, or the local grocer cannot and do not wish to first complete a computer science degree or spend a lot of money on external service providers. This is where we come into play. We have developed a system that we call Guided Hosting. Through strict standardization and a precisely defined range of services, we can offer solutions that are affordable and understandable even for non-experts. We use exclusively free and open-source platforms and software for this, just like for all our offerings. This service is still under development, but we will continuously provide new components and solutions for you. It should also be noted that we can only meet a limited part of the requirements. We believe that our valued customers primarily place importance on security, privacy, and manageable complexity. This is exactly where we excel.

Our Organization

This organization is actually something like a social and economic experiment. Allow me to introduce myself, my name is Markus Eicher and I founded it. At the current moment, it has no legal form whatsoever. At least none that is intended. Unfortunately, my health is too poor for me to think about an effective establishment. Nevertheless, there are ideas and, as I believe, certainly a demand as well. SwissHostedSolutions will never become a profit-oriented company. If my health allows, I might eventually turn it into an association or a non-profit organization. Currently, I can't work regularly at all. This project is what I want to do when I (hopefully) feel better someday. Until then, I can do something step by step when I'm feeling good and stop when I'm not. In order to show my gratitude for the long-standing support from the state, I will offer my services to municipalities, cantons, or even the federal government free of charge. However, I can only take on tasks that do not have tight schedules, as sometimes I barely manage to get by for days and can't even manage my urgently needed walk. Public authorities are welcome to contact me if they have something that I can do for them within the scope I described here. I will be happy to do this pro bono, but also without time pressure. At the same time, there are also contacts with potential partners who would like to get the operation here off the ground. We are currently working on the statutes for an association, but until then, it is not much more than a declaration of intent on my part.
