What it is about

Secure access to AI — no big cloud required

Local AI with open-source models — here too with a focus on privacy and data protection. We want to help small companies, associations and especially our municipalities and public administration find secure and confidential access to AI.

Rent our hardware at fair prices for your projects. Your data, your hardware: when your project is done, we clean up and reset everything — ready for the next customer.

Your data leaves neither Switzerland nor the machine it is processed on. No cloud dependency, no sharing with third parties — and no models quietly trained on your data.
