Project

Hosted NextCloud.

Self-service recipes and Infrastructure as Code for your own NextCloud — documented step by step and fully open source.

Update — July 2026

Our NextCloud is live — fully as code

In production: our own NextCloud now runs entirely as Infrastructure as Code. We are currently preparing the recipes behind it for publication.

To support day-to-day operations we also use a locally hosted open-source AI that knows our own operations documentation — again without any external services. Made possible by resources from our Local AI project.

What it is about

Your cloud, under your control

We develop self-service recipes and Infrastructure-as-Code implementations that let you set up and run your own NextCloud instance — documented step by step and fully open source.

Self-hosting should not be a hurdle. Our recipes guide you from choosing the environment through installation to backups and updates. What we document, we run ourselves — on Swiss soil.

In a later step we will also offer NextCloud as community-driven hosting — deliberately not a managed service with SLAs: as an association we do not want to become a service provider. Instead, our members and partners actively help others with operation and support. No SLA, but plenty of heart and know-how.

The project in brief

Status: our own instance in production — the recipes are being prepared for publication
Area: self-hosting
Software: NextCloud, fully open source
Hosting: Switzerland only

What to expect

  • ✓Community instead of contract — help comes from members and partners. No SLA, but plenty of heart and know-how.
